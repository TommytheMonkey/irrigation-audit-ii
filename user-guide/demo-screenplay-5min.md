Irrigation Audit — 5-min demo screenplay                                                                          
                                                                                                                  
  ▎ Runtime target: ~4:45. Pace is tight — read fast but don't rush.                                                
  ▎ Record on the production app with Tommy's House / Cascia Demo seed data. Use a real iPhone for mobile segments.
                                                                                                                    
  ---                                                                                                               
  SCENE 1 — Cold open (0:00 – 0:20)                                                                                 
                                                                                                                    
  ON SCREEN: Slow pan across a messy desk: clipboard with crumpled audit form, iPad, coffee. Cut to a spreadsheet on
   a laptop with 200 rows of hand-typed parts.                                                                      
                                                            
  VOICEOVER:                                                                                                        
                                                            
  ▎ Irrigation audits are a nightmare. A tech walks the property with a clipboard. Back at the office someone       
  ▎ retypes it into a spreadsheet. A week later the estimator prices it, a week after that the client sees the 
  ▎ proposal. Quotes take a month. Half the data is lost in translation.                                            
                                                            
  TEXT OVERLAY: fading in: "There's a better way."                                                                  
   
  ---                                                                                                               
  SCENE 2 — Dashboard (0:20 – 1:00)                         
                                                                                                                    
  ON SCREEN: Fade into the app on a laptop — irrigation-audit.takeo.co — the dashboard. Property cards in a grid.
                                                                                                                    
  VOICEOVER:                                                
                                                                                                                    
  ▎ This is Irrigation Audit. It's a field-first tool for landscape companies. The dashboard shows every property   
  ▎ you manage — synced from Monday.com in one click.
                                                                                                                    
  ACTION: Click the Map toggle. Property pins drop onto a map of Austin with property markers clustered.            
   
  VOICEOVER:                                                                                                        
                                                            
  ▎ List view, map view. Filter by property manager, audit status, or source. Search is instant.                    
   
  ACTION: Type "cascia" into the search bar. One card remains.                                                      
                                                            
  VOICEOVER:                                                                                                        
                                                            
  ▎ Let's look at Cascia Hall.                                                                                      
   
  ACTION: Click View on the property card.                                                                          
                                                            
  ---                                                                                                               
  SCENE 3 — System profile (1:00 – 1:40)                    
                                                                                                                    
  ON SCREEN: Property detail page. Tabs: "Cascia Demo (as-built)". Sub-tabs: Overview / Parts in use / Files.
                                                                                                                    
  VOICEOVER:                                                
                                                                                                                    
  ▎ Every property has a system profile — controllers, water sources, backflow, zones, parts. The source of truth   
  ▎ for what's actually in the ground.
                                                                                                                    
  ACTION: Scroll through the Overview. Show controllers (Rain Bird ESP-2WIRE, 10 stations, two-wire), water source  
  (POC city meter), backflow (Wilkins 975XL2), and the zone list.
                                                                                                                    
  ACTION: Click the Parts in use sub-tab. Scroll through 29 parts — Rain Bird 5004-PC rotors, XFS dripline, PGA     
  valves.
                                                                                                                    
  VOICEOVER:                                                

  ▎ Every part is matched to the vendor catalog automatically. See that green check? That's our catalog matcher     
  ▎ linking this line to the real Rain Bird SKU.
                                                                                                                    
  ACTION: Hover a "✓ Catalog" badge to show the tooltip with rainbird: Rain Bird 5004-PC 3.0.                       
  
  ---                                                                                                               
  SCENE 4 — AI magic (1:40 – 2:25)                          
                                                                                                                    
  ON SCREEN: Click + Add system → wizard opens with two options: "Start blank" or "From zone-chart photo."
                                                                                                                    
  VOICEOVER:                                                
                                                                                                                    
  ▎ Setting up a new system used to take an hour of data entry. Now? Point your phone at the chart inside the       
  ▎ controller door.
                                                                                                                    
  ACTION: Select the photo option. Pick a phone photo of an actual controller zone chart. Click Extract & create.   
  Loading spinner: "Extracting…"
                                                                                                                    
  ON SCREEN: After ~15 seconds, toast pops: "Front Yard added with 10 zones extracted."                             
  
  ACTION: Click the new system's tab. Show the 10 zones populated with names, types, valve details.                 
                                                            
  VOICEOVER:                                                                                                        
                                                            
  ▎ Claude read the chart, parsed every zone, and filled it in. Upload an Excel takeoff or a full as-built PDF      
  ▎ instead — same thing happens. Magic.
                                                                                                                    
  ---                                                       
  SCENE 5 — The audit (on iPad) (2:25 – 3:20)
                                                                                                                    
  ON SCREEN: Cut to iPad screen recording. App is already open on the same property. User taps Start New Audit.
                                                                                                                    
  VOICEOVER:                                                
                                                                                                                    
  ▎ Time to audit. The new audit starts from the system profile — all 10 zones pre-populated. The tech doesn't      
  ▎ re-enter the layout; they just walk and tap.
                                                                                                                    
  ACTION (on iPad): Tap Zone 3. The zone page loads with quick-pick chips: "Broken head," "Missing nozzle," "Leaking
   valve," "Clogged emitter."
                                                                                                                    
  ACTION: Tap "Broken head." Tap severity High. Tap the camera icon → take a photo of a grass patch with a broken   
  sprinkler head. Photo appears as a thumbnail instantly.
                                                                                                                    
  VOICEOVER:                                                

  ▎ Every photo is compressed on-device before upload — an iPhone photo drops from 10 megabytes to under one, so    
  ▎ uploads finish in seconds even on LTE at the back of the property.
                                                                                                                    
  ACTION: Move through zones 4, 5, 6 quickly. Add one finding per zone, occasional photo.                           
   
  ACTION: Tap Complete audit. Summary screen appears with severity stats and findings by category.                  
                                                                                                                  
  ---                                                                                                               
  SCENE 6 — The proposal (3:20 – 4:10)                                                                            
                                                                                                                    
  ON SCREEN: Cut back to the laptop. On the audit summary, click Export to Sheets.
                                                                                                                    
  VOICEOVER:                                                                                                      
                                                                                                                    
  ▎ The estimator gets a Google Sheet. Four tabs: summary, findings, parts, totals. Fill in prices, hit sync, and   
  ▎ they flow straight back to the audit.
                                                                                                                    
  ACTION: Show the Google Sheet opening in a new tab with the four tabs visible. Zoom in on the "Findings" tab for a
   beat.
                                                                                                                    
  ACTION: Switch back to the app. Go to the property's Files tab. Click Generate report.                            
   
  ON SCREEN: Wizard inline — title "Cascia Hall Proposal," audit picked, all sections toggled on.                   
                                                                                                                  
  ACTION: Click Generate PDF. Loading spinner. ~15 seconds later toast appears with a Download action.              
                                                                                                                  
  ACTION: Click Download. PDF opens full-screen.                                                                    
                                                                                                                  
  VOICEOVER:                                                                                                        
   
  ▎ The report comes out branded — your colors, your logo, your fonts. Cover page, system summary, every zone, parts
  ▎  list, findings with photos inline, severity, recommendations. Ready to send to the client the same day.      
                                                                                                                    
  ACTION: Scroll through the PDF slowly — cover with the Takeo yellow banner, page 2 system summary, page 3 the     
  parts table with repeating headers across page break, page 4 findings with photos. Zoom in on the footer: "Powered
   by Takeo by Takeoff Monkey" with the little space monkey.

  ---
  Production notes
                  
  - B-roll ideas if you have time: tech walking through tall grass holding iPad, close-up of a broken sprinkler head
   with water arcing wrong, coffee steam over the laptop.                                                           
  - Keep the laptop cursor small and steady. Slow, deliberate clicks read as confidence.
  - Record each scene separately and stitch — one take start-to-finish on a live app is a recipe for pain.          
  - Pre-seed the demo data: npx tsx --env-file=.env scripts/seed-cascia.ts --property "Cascia" if you want the      
  profile populated on a specific property before filming.                                                          
  - The Magic Fill scene is the money shot — if you can only keep 30 seconds, keep that one.                        
  - Background music: something calm/productive (Hammock, Tycho, Jon Hopkins — nothing with a drop). Volume low     
  under VO.                                                                                                         
  - Font for overlays: Montserrat (matches the app's brand font). 48pt for the hook text, 32pt for end card.
