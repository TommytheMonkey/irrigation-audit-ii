  ---
  Production notes
                  
  - No desk shots, no problem-statement buildup. The 60-sec cut earns attention by being in motion from frame one.
  - The zone-chart photo has to be real — a blurry, handwritten, obviously-field photo sells the magic more than a  
  clean one does.                                                                                                   
  - VO is ~18 words per section — lean, factual, no adjectives.                                                     
  - Sound: uptempo but understated — something like Explosions in the Sky's quieter moments, or even just ambient   
  Ableton presets. No drops.                                                                                        
  - Cutting plan: shoot the scenes at 5-min length, then speed-ramp the typing / clicking parts 2–3× in post to fit 
  the timing. Voiceover stays at normal speed.                                                                      
  - The iPad → laptop cut at 0:35 is the hardest to make feel seamless — match the color temperature in grading so
  the devices feel part of the same world.                                                                          
                                                            
  Same end-card as the long version, same URL, same monkey.
